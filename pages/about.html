About

A World of Dark Comedy creates a festival of crazy, bizarre and extraordinary tales by short filmmakers, presenting a world of the worst things that can happen in human interactions. It is the most fun you can have under the cover of ‘dark’ness.

Our mission

We strive for excellence in curating and presenting comedy films that change the way we view the world. We celebrate new and independent filmmakers, their comic masterpieces and their excellent sense of humour.

Above all, we create a vibrant, stimulating, inspiring environment for filmmakers and audiences, and bring laughter, thought and joy to people.

Our collections of comedy films will have you smile, grin, wonder, laugh and, most of all, consider their wild and wonderfully bizarre scenarios. Enjoy!

Over The Fence Comedy Film Festival

A World of Dark Comedy is chuffed with our collaborative relationship with our ‘bro’ festival, the Over The Fence Comedy Film Festival.

Voces

Voces Arts Networking Group is the parent group of both the Over The Fence Comedy Film Festival and A World of Dark Comedy. We are a not-for-profit organisation operating out of Perth for comedy expression and production worldwide.
