Call for entries for both the Over The Fence Comedy Film Festival and A World of Dark Comedy.

  1. Format should be online screeners.
  2. Short film, long-form short, or short film series.
  3. Any comic genre: black, slapstick, irreverent, romantic, political, subversive, absurd… etc.
  4. Any form: drama, doco, animation, experimental.
  5. Completed since 2015.
  6. And it must have an excellent sense of humour!
