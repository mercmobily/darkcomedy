## WoDC 1–4 and 6: the old archive

Rules applied everywhere, so every year now reads the same way:

* **Country and length** are written “Place / minutes:seconds”: `(swd 10.43)` → `Sweden / 10:43`, `8 mins / Qld` → `QLD / 8 min`,
  `5.43 / WA` → `WA / 5:43`. Australian states in capitals (VIC, QLD); other countries in full (New Zealand, not NZ).
* **Credits** are written out with one dash: `Dir –` → `Director –`, `Prod –` → `Producer –`, `Scpt –` → `Script –`,
  `Co Pro` → `Co-producers`, `Key Cast` → `Cast`, `Cast Charlotte…` → `Cast – Charlotte…`, `Scpt -Rick` → `Script – Rick`.
* **“And others”** is written ` +` everywhere (the old site mixed `+`, `…`, `..` and `++`).
* **Lines of dialogue** (2019) were mixed into the synopses with odd punctuation (`right?.`, `go..`). They are now the film’s quote, as in the 2024 and 2025 programmes.
* **Australian spelling** in synopses: realise, fulfil, theatre, humour, favour. Quotes are left as spoken.
